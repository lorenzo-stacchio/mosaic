        Why MOSAIC exists

        MOSAIC was made by Lorenzo Stacchio, Research Fellow in Computer Science at the University of Macerata. His research joins extended reality and artificial intelligence with cultural heritage, education, the creative industries and manufacturing, so every paper raises the same question: which venue will a computer scientist and a humanist both respect?

        Answering it meant cross-checking SCImago category by category. MOSAIC does that crossing for you, and is shared for every team that works between disciplines.

        lorenzo-stacchio.github.io Source code on GitHub

        Built on SCImago

        Every quartile, SJR value, H-index and subject category shown here comes from the SCImago Journal & Country Rank, developed by the SCImago Research Group from Scopus data. MOSAIC adds no ranking of its own: it reorganises SCImago to answer one question its pages leave open, namely which venues belong to several fields at once.

        If these indicators inform your choice, please cite SCImago:

        SCImago (n.d.). SJR: SCImago Journal & Country Rank [Portal]. Retrieved from https://www.scimagojr.com
